Why local, and honest by design

Why it must run on the box

Supplier contracts, margins, and sourcing are trade secrets — no operator ships them to a cloud LLM. FSMA 204 makes traceability a legal mandate. Always-on + private = only possible on the GB10.

Real vs modeled — stated, never blurred

Real: recalls, health violations, distributor history, buildings. Modeled: supplier→site links (the FSMA-204 gap itself), the operator's portfolio. We label it — an instrument, not an oracle.
